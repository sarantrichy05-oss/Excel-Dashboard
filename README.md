This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales data using Microsoft Excel. The project involves data cleaning, transformation, descriptive analysis, and interactive dashboard creation to generate actionable business insights.
The goal was to evaluate sales performance, customer behavior, product trends, and discount impact to support data-driven decision-making.

The objectives of this project is:
To analyze overall sales performance across products and categories
To clean and transform raw transactional data for accurate analysis.
To evaluate pricing, discount, and quantity relationships.
To identify high-value customers using loyalty segmentation.
To analyze stock availability and product demand trends.
To generate descriptive statistics for business insights.
To create interactive dashboards for performance monitoring.

Tools & Techniques used are Microsoft Excel, Data Cleaning & Transformation, IF, ISBLANK Functions, XLOOKUP & VLOOKUP,Pivot Tables, Descriptive Statistics (Mean, Median, Mode, Standard Deviation) and Dashboard Creation.

Insights are high-loyalty customers contribute significantly to revenue. Certain product categories outperform others in sales volume. Excessive discounts reduce profitability margins. Stock management plays a crucial role in maintaining sales consistency. Historical purchase patterns help identify future sales opportunities.
