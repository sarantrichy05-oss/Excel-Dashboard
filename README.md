##  Project Objective

The objective of this capstone project is to analyze e-commerce sales data using Microsoft Excel, perform data cleaning and transformation, apply descriptive analysis techniques, and generate actionable insights through visualizations and dashboards to support business decision-making.

---

## Dataset Description

The dataset consists of multiple sheets including:

- Sales Fact
- Product Dimension
- Customer Dimension

It contains:

- Transactional sales records  
- Product details  
- Pricing and discount information  
- Quantity sold  
- Stock availability  
- Customer purchase history  

This structured dataset supports analytical reporting and performance evaluation.

---

## Column Description

| Column Name   | Description |
|--------------|------------|
| Customer ID  | Unique identifier for each customer |
| Product ID   | Unique identifier for each product |
| Unit Price   | Price per unit of the product |
| Quantity     | Number of units sold |
| Discount     | Discount applied to the product |
| Total Amount | Final sales value after discount |
| Category     | Product category |
| Stock        | Available inventory quantity |

## Key Insights

- High-value (Platinum and Gold) customers contribute a significant portion of total revenue, indicating strong repeat purchase behavior.

- Certain product categories consistently generate higher sales volumes, making them key revenue drivers.

- Discount percentage has a direct impact on sales quantity; moderate discounts improve sales performance, while excessive discounts reduce profitability.

- Stock availability directly affects revenue generation — low stock levels limit potential sales opportunities.

- Sales distribution analysis shows variation in pricing behavior across different categories.

- Descriptive statistics reveal noticeable variation in unit price and quantity sold, indicating uneven product performance.

- Customer purchase patterns help identify loyal customers and predict future sales opportunities.
- 
## Recommendations

- Focus targeted marketing campaigns on Platinum and Gold loyalty customers to maximize revenue retention.

- Maintain adequate stock levels for high-demand and high-performing products to prevent revenue loss.

- Optimize discount strategies to balance sales growth and profit margins rather than offering excessive discounts.

- Promote top-performing product categories through strategic advertising and bundled offers.

- Implement customer segmentation strategies to personalize marketing efforts.

- Use historical sales trends to forecast demand and improve inventory planning.

- Monitor pricing strategies regularly to ensure competitiveness and profitability.
- 
## Conclusion

This capstone project demonstrates the practical application of Microsoft Excel in performing data cleaning, transformation, descriptive analysis, and dashboard creation for e-commerce sales data.

The analysis identified key revenue drivers, customer loyalty patterns, pricing behavior trends, and the impact of discounts and stock levels on overall performance.

By leveraging structured data analysis techniques, the project provides actionable insights that support strategic decision-making in sales optimization, customer retention, and inventory management.

Overall, this project highlights how data-driven approaches can improve operational efficiency, enhance profitability, and support long-term business growth.
